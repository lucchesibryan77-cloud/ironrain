## Direction contract

THESIS: A civic institution for small-business uptime — the site behaves like a stamped public record, not a startup poster. Refuses the category default (dark cyber editorial with neon accent) and its predictable opposite (generic corporate template).

OWN-WORLD: Warm sand fields (#F8F3E9 / #EFE7D5), deep dusk-navy masses (#203349), terracotta clay committed at page scale (#B4542F / deep #96421F) on the closing band and primary actions. Zilla Slab headlines in sentence case (never all-caps shouting), Libre Franklin body/UI, system mono only for measured data (prices, SLAs). 1px sand-tinted rules instead of shadows; flat plates, no gradients, no glass, no mono-costume kickers, no eyebrow labels above headings, no numbered sections, no stats-hero grid, no stripe/grid textures.

STORY: A nervous owner lands here from a referral or an insurance panic and reads a firm that files things: names, dates, prices, response times. Proof arrives as records (an operating-record card, a client register, a dated ledger), and the ask is one clear action — book the baseline call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sand ground. Top utility strip in dusk (location, office line, 24/7 client line). Paper nav with slab wordmark. Twelve-column hero: seven columns of slab headline "We keep New Mexico small business running — and keep the bad guys out." with clay weight on "running", a Franklin dek, clay-deep primary button + ink outline secondary; five columns an "Operating Record" card — small-caps title bar, ledger rows (endpoints under watch, median IR response, uptime), sun-over-mesa geometric SVG, 24/7 line footer. Credentials line under a hairline.

FORM: Grounded candidate #7 (desert-modern civic institution), assigned by concept-seed bd5fa62e; probe timed out unanswered → assignment binds. Raises: (ascii) clay commits at page scale, not accents; (darkroom) service process rendered as fixed stations with completion states; (alphabet-storm) one monumental slab message per viewport, max 6rem; (gameboy) tap targets ≥44px, contrast ≥4.5:1; (papercut) flat overlap instead of diffuse shadow; (merz) credentials strip treated as filed clippings.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
