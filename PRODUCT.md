# PRODUCT.md — Iron Rain SecOps Solutions

## What this is
Marketing site for Iron Rain SecOps Solutions, LLC — an Albuquerque, NM consulting firm.
Four practices: Managed IT, Security Operations (24/7 SOC), Compliance & Risk (NIST/HIPAA/CMMC/PCI),
Web Development & Design. Productized packages: RainWatch MDR, IronBaseline, StormCell IR Retainer.

## Visitor and their scene (drives every visual call)
A New Mexico small-business owner or office manager — dental practice, machine shop, law firm,
roofing crew. 35–60 years old. Finds the site via referral, Google, or a compliance panic
(insurance renewal, CMMC clause). Checks it on a phone in a truck or an office PC under
fluorescent light. They are deciding one thing: *are these grown-ups who answer the phone?*

A second visitor matters: a verification reviewer at a bank/perks program checking the
business is real. Their scan: address, phone, services, people, a pulse of activity.

## What the surface must prove
Trust through specificity — named people, real prices, dated work, written artifacts.
Every claim is concrete or it doesn't ship. The opposite failure: looking like a startup
mood board (dark neon "cyber" template) or a generic WordPress SMB theme.

## Untouchable truth
All copy/content in src/lib/* is the product truth (placeholder facts flagged for user swap).
Company: founded 2017, Albuquerque HQ, 505 area code, NM/AZ/CO/TX service area.
Tone: plain-English operator voice. No fear-mongering, no hype adjectives.

## Money moment
Book a baseline call → /contact (form + phone + address). Every page routes there once, clearly.

## Stack constraints
Next.js 16 App Router, Tailwind v4 tokens in globals.css, static SSG, next/font/google only.
No backend (contact form = zod + mailto until wired). Team portraits hotlink randomuser.me
(placeholder; user must replace with real photos).
